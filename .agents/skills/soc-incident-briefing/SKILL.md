# Soc Incident Briefing

1. Separate observations, hypotheses, alternatives, confidence, and recommended analyst actions.
2. Do not auto-block or execute response actions.
3. Preserve deterministic risk score and model provenance.
4. Include known limitations and what additional evidence would change the assessment.
