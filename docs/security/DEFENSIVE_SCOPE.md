# Defensive Scope & Safety Policy

## Operational Invariants
1. **Strictly Defensive**: The platform is built to detect, classify, and triage threats in synthetic and local data.
2. **No Offensive Scanning**: No capability to target, probe, or scan third-party hosts.
3. **No Evasion Tooling**: No CAPTCHA bypass, anti-bot evasion, or fingerprint spoofing.
4. **Local Execution**: All evaluations execute locally against seeded synthetic datasets.
